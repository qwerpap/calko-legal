Privacy Policy

Calko: AR Drawing Lessons · Updated September 29, 2026

Calko helps you trace drawings onto paper with your phone's camera and learn to draw layer by layer. It is built to keep your drawings and photos on your device.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera. The live camera image is used only to show the drawing over your paper. It is processed on your device and is never recorded to a server or uploaded. When you snap a finished drawing, that one photo is saved in the app's own storage on your phone.
  • Your photos. A photo you pick to trace is turned into line layers on your device with Apple's Core Image and Vision frameworks. It is not uploaded and no AI service receives it.
  • Sketchbook and timelapses are stored only inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Quiz answers (what you want to draw, how often you draw, who it is for) personalize your plan.

What is processed by service providers

  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Adapty (adapty.io) processes subscription status and anonymous app usage events (for example: which onboarding step was reached, that the paywall was shown, your quiz answers) to show prices, manage subscriptions and measure the app. It uses a random app identifier, not your name or email. If you arrive from an Apple Search Ads ad, Adapty may receive the campaign attribution Apple provides.

What we don't do

  • No account or login. No advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps, no selling of data.
  • Calko is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off camera access in iOS Settings at any time — Light box mode works without it. You can delete drawings inside the app or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. For questions or deletion requests about data held by Adapty, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com.
