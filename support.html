Calko: AR Drawing Lessons · Updated September 29, 2026

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we usually answer within a day. The “Write to us” button in Calko's settings adds your app version automatically.

Quick answers

  • How do I set up? Put the phone on a glass or a stack of books above your paper, camera facing down. Lock the drawing once it's placed.
  • My hand hides the lines. Tap the eye button to hide the drawing for 3 seconds and check your sheet.
  • No camera? Use Light box: lay thin paper on the screen and trace.
  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Calko.
